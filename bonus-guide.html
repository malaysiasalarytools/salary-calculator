Malaysia bonus guide 2026

Understand how employee bonuses work in Malaysia, including annual bonus, performance bonus, EPF, SOCSO, EIS, PCB tax, pro-rated bonus and common payroll treatment.

Bonus Quick Summary

A bonus is an additional payment on top of normal salary. The payroll treatment depends on what the payment actually is: an annual bonus, incentive, commission or another type of additional remuneration may not be treated the same under every statutory scheme.

EPFBonus and incentive payments are generally subject to EPF contribution.
SOCSOAn annual bonus is excluded from PERKESO wages, while incentives can be contributable wages.
EISThe EIS Act excludes annual bonus from the definition of wages.
PCBHASiL treats bonus or incentive as additional remuneration for monthly tax deduction purposes.
Important: The label used on a payslip is not always enough to determine statutory treatment. Payroll should consider the actual nature of the payment and the rules of each scheme.

What Is a Bonus in Malaysia?

A bonus is an additional payment made to an employee on top of normal salary. It may be linked to company performance, individual performance, profit, a festive period, a contractual formula or the employer's discretion.

For payroll purposes, it is important to distinguish a true annual bonus from an incentive, commission or other payment. KWSP, PERKESO and HASiL do not necessarily classify every additional payment in the same way.

Total Cash Pay for the Month = Normal Salary + Bonus / Additional PaymentStatutory deductions are then determined using the rules that apply to each payment and scheme.

Is an Annual Bonus Mandatory in Malaysia?

There is no single rule under the Employment Act 1955 that gives every private-sector employee an automatic fixed annual bonus. Whether a bonus is payable can depend on the employment contract, collective agreement, company bonus scheme, written policy or the conditions attached to the payment.

The Employment Act's definition of “wages” specifically excludes an annual bonus or part of an annual bonus. That definition, however, does not by itself decide whether an employer has a separate contractual obligation to pay a promised bonus.

Practical check: If your offer letter says “guaranteed 1-month contractual bonus”, that is different from wording such as “bonus may be paid at the company's discretion subject to performance”. Always read the actual bonus clause.

Is Bonus Subject to EPF / KWSP?

Generally, yes. KWSP lists both bonus and incentive as payments subject to EPF contribution. This means an employee's bonus can increase the EPF contribution for the month in which it is paid.

ItemGeneral 2026 PositionWhat It Means
BonusSubject to EPFIncluded in contribution wages where the employee is liable to contribute.
IncentiveSubject to EPFKWSP also lists incentives as a component of wages.
Malaysian / PR below 60Common statutory rate: employee 11%; employer 13% at RM5,000 and below, 12% above RM5,000Use the applicable Third Schedule rather than assuming exact percentage multiplication for all wage levels.

KWSP's current guidance states that employers generally use the Third Schedule contribution table, and exact percentage calculation is not used for ordinary wages up to RM20,000.

Special bonus point: The current Third Schedule notes that where an employee's normal monthly wages are RM5,000 or below and a bonus causes that month's wages to exceed RM5,000, the employer contribution is calculated at the 13% rate for that month.

Is Bonus Subject to SOCSO and EIS?

For a genuine annual bonus, the treatment differs from EPF. PERKESO's definition of wages under the Employees' Social Security Act excludes annual bonus, and the Employment Insurance System Act also excludes annual bonus from wages.

Is Bonus Subject to PCB / Income Tax?

Yes, bonus is generally taxable employment income. For PCB / Monthly Tax Deduction purposes, HASiL treats a bonus or incentive as additional remuneration.

The PCB on a bonus is therefore not simply a flat percentage of the bonus. The additional-remuneration calculation takes account of the employee's remuneration and tax position for the year, including accumulated remuneration, applicable deductions / relief information and PCB already deducted.

Bonus PCB ≠ Bonus × One Fixed Tax RateHASiL's additional-remuneration method recalculates the annual tax position and determines the extra PCB attributable to the additional remuneration.

This is why a large year-end bonus can cause the PCB deduction in that particular month to be much higher than in a normal salary month.

For tax details, see the PCB Guide and Income Tax Guide.

Do not use one rule for every scheme. EPF, SOCSO, EIS and PCB are governed by different laws and definitions. The same RM5,000 bonus can therefore have different treatment under each system.

How Is a Pro-Rated Bonus Calculated?

There is no universal statutory pro-rated bonus formula that applies to every employer. If the company scheme states that a one-month annual bonus is earned proportionately according to completed eligible service, a common contractual formula may look like this:

Pro-Rated Bonus = Full Bonus Entitlement × Eligible Service ÷ Full Service PeriodUse the exact formula and eligibility rules stated in the employment contract, collective agreement or bonus policy.

Example: An employee has a monthly basic salary of RM4,800 and the company awards a 1-month bonus. If the written scheme provides for 9 months of eligible service out of 12 months, the illustrative gross bonus would be RM4,800 × 9 ÷ 12 = RM3,600.

However, a company may instead use completed months, confirmation date, joining cut-off date, performance rating, unpaid-leave adjustment or other conditions. Do not assume the 9/12 formula applies unless the scheme provides for it.

Malaysia Bonus Payroll Examples

Example 1: RM5,000 salary + RM5,000 annual bonus

An employee receives normal monthly salary of RM5,000 and a genuine RM5,000 annual bonus in the same month.

  • EPF: the bonus is included in EPF contribution wages. Under the current Third Schedule note, where normal monthly wages are RM5,000 or below and the bonus pushes that month's wages above RM5,000, the employer contribution remains calculated at 13%.
  • SOCSO: the genuine annual bonus is excluded from wages for SOCSO contribution purposes.
  • EIS: annual bonus is excluded from wages under Act 800.
  • PCB: the RM5,000 bonus is additional remuneration and may cause an additional PCB deduction for the month.

Example 2: Monthly sales incentive

An employee receives RM4,000 salary plus a RM600 monthly sales incentive based on targets achieved.

  • The payment should not automatically be treated as an “annual bonus”.
  • KWSP lists incentives as EPF-contributable payments.
  • PERKESO lists incentive allowances among payments treated as wages for SOCSO purposes.
  • For PCB, incentives can fall within additional remuneration.

Example 3: Contractual one-month year-end bonus

If an employment contract expressly promises a one-month contractual bonus subject to stated eligibility conditions, payroll should follow those contractual conditions and then apply the statutory contribution and PCB rules separately.

What Should Appear on the Bonus Payslip?

  • Normal salary and bonus / incentive should be clearly distinguishable.
  • The gross bonus amount should agree with the contract, bonus letter or approved payroll record.
  • EPF attributable to the month's contributable wages should be correctly reflected.
  • SOCSO and EIS contribution wages should not simply include a genuine annual bonus without checking the statutory definition.
  • Additional PCB arising from the bonus should be shown clearly where applicable.
  • The final net amount should reconcile to the bank payment.

Common Bonus Payroll Mistakes

  • Assuming all bonuses are exempt from statutory contributions.
  • Applying SOCSO / EIS annual-bonus treatment to a recurring incentive without checking what the payment actually represents.
  • Forgetting that bonus is subject to EPF.
  • Using 11%, 12% or 13% as a simple exact EPF multiplication when the Third Schedule table should be used.
  • Calculating PCB on bonus using a flat tax percentage instead of the additional-remuneration method.
  • Assuming every employee is legally guaranteed a one-month annual bonus.
  • Pro-rating a contractual bonus without checking the scheme's service and eligibility rules.
  • Combining salary, incentive and annual bonus into one unclear payslip line.

Bonus Checklist for Employees

  • Read the bonus clause in your offer letter, employment contract or company policy.
  • Confirm whether the payment is an annual bonus, incentive or commission.
  • Check the gross bonus and any pro-rating calculation.
  • Compare the EPF contribution with the applicable KWSP schedule.
  • Check whether a genuine annual bonus has been excluded from SOCSO / EIS wages where appropriate.
  • Expect PCB to vary because bonus is additional remuneration.
  • Keep the bonus letter and payslip for tax and payroll records.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is bonus subject to EPF in Malaysia?

Generally yes. KWSP lists bonus as a payment subject to EPF contribution. The contribution should follow the applicable Third Schedule.

Is annual bonus subject to SOCSO?

A genuine annual bonus is excluded from the definition of wages under the Employees' Social Security Act. However, incentives, commissions and other payments may have different treatment.

Is annual bonus subject to EIS?

The Employment Insurance System Act 2017 excludes annual bonus from the definition of wages.

Is bonus taxable in Malaysia?

Yes. Bonus is generally employment income, and HASiL treats bonus or incentive as additional remuneration for PCB / MTD purposes.

Why is PCB much higher in my bonus month?

Bonus is additional remuneration. HASiL's method considers the employee's annualised tax position and determines additional PCB rather than applying one flat rate to the bonus.

Is every employee entitled to a one-month bonus?

No universal one-month bonus applies to every employee. Entitlement depends on the employment contract, collective agreement, company scheme or other applicable terms.

Can my employer pro-rate my bonus?

It depends on the bonus scheme. If the contract or policy allows pro-rating based on eligible service, the employer should apply the stated formula and conditions consistently.

Does bonus affect employer EPF rate if salary is RM5,000?

KWSP's current Third Schedule specifically notes that where normal monthly wages are RM5,000 or below and a bonus pushes the month's wages above RM5,000, the employer contribution is calculated at 13% for that month.

Is a performance incentive the same as annual bonus for SOCSO?

Not necessarily. PERKESO excludes annual bonus but includes incentive allowances in contributable wages, so the actual nature of the payment should be checked.
