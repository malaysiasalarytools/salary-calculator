How Salary Is Calculated in Malaysia

Calculating your take-home pay means subtracting statutory deductions from your gross monthly salary. In Malaysia, the main deductions are usually EPF, SOCSO, EIS and PCB.

Net Salary = Gross Salary − Total DeductionsTotal Deductions = EPF + SOCSO + EIS + PCB

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this salary calculator include EPF, SOCSO, EIS and PCB?

Yes. It estimates monthly take-home pay after EPF, SOCSO, EIS and estimated PCB deductions based on your gross monthly salary.

Why is my actual salary different from the calculator result?

Actual payroll may vary because of tax relief claims, allowances, bonuses, overtime, unpaid leave and employer payroll settings.

How is PCB tax calculated in Malaysia?

PCB is a monthly tax deduction administered by LHDN. The amount depends on chargeable income, tax reliefs and personal circumstances.

Can I calculate annual salary from monthly salary?

Yes. The calculator provides monthly and annual breakdowns based on the gross monthly salary entered.

Do bonuses and allowances affect take-home pay?

Yes. Bonuses, commissions, allowances and overtime may increase statutory deductions and PCB tax.

What tax reliefs can reduce PCB deductions?

Common reliefs include EPF contributions, insurance, lifestyle expenses, education fees, medical expenses and child reliefs.
